KeyUsed byNotes
APP_NAMEWeb shell title, emailsCustomer product name
PRIMARY_COLORCSS --brand-primaryHex
LOGO_URLNav / loginHTTPS URL
CUSTOM_DOMAINProd redirects, cookiesApex or www
DEV_CUSTOM_DOMAINDev environmentUsually dev.{CUSTOM_DOMAIN}

Do not commit customer logos or secrets into source. Agents should update CSS tokens and copy, not bake secrets into the repo.
